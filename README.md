What it does
SmartBudget Manager is an Excel workbook for tracking monthly income and expenses. It groups spending by category, calculates totals and monthly balance, displays a dashboard with charts, and highlights overspending when a budget limit is exceeded.
Workbook sheets
Dashboard: monthly balance, total income, total expenses, and charts.
Transactions: enter dated income and expense records.
Categories: predefined categories such as Food, Transport, Rent, Health, and Education.
Requirements
Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021, Microsoft 365, or compatible spreadsheet software.
The workbook file: `SmartBudget\_Manager.xlsx`.
Quick start
Open `SmartBudget\_Manager.xlsx`.
Go to Transactions and enter each transaction on a new row.
For income, enter the date, description, and amount in the Income column.
For an expense, enter the date and description, select a category, and enter the amount in the Expense column.
Open Dashboard to review totals, balance, and available charts.
Save your own copy regularly.
Important notes
Do not overwrite formula cells.
Confirm that the transaction date, category, and amount are correct.
Budget-limit controls and exact cell locations depend on the actual workbook version; follow the labels provided in the file.
Keep a backup and do not share the workbook if it contains private financial information.
